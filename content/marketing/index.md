# BusinessOS by E-console

> E-console on econsole.in — Powering Digital Possibilities. Run your business on BusinessOS: billing, inventory, staff, and projects for Indian businesses.

BusinessOS is an all-in-one business management platform built for Indian shops, contractors, architects, and service businesses. Manage GST billing, inventory, udhaar (credit ledger), staff attendance, expenses, and multi-branch operations from web, Android, or Windows.

## Key capabilities

- **GST billing & POS** — Fast invoicing, barcode scan, hold bills, print templates, offers at checkout
- **Inventory** — Stock tracking, purchases, returns, low-stock alerts, label printing
- **Udhaar ledger** — Customer credit tracking with payment history
- **Staff & attendance** — Roles, payslips, branch transfers
- **Multi-module** — Shop, contractor (BOQ, material), architect (stages), service (appointments, contracts)
- **Offline Android** — Billing works without internet; sync when connected
