# Privacy Policy — BusinessOS by E-console

> How E-console (econsole.in) collects, uses, and protects your data when you use BusinessOS.

Last updated: September 2026.

## Overview

E-console ("we", "us") operates BusinessOS at [https://www.econsole.in](https://www.econsole.in). This policy describes how we handle information when you create an account, use our web application, or use our Android and Windows clients.

## Information we collect

- **Account data** — Name, email address, and organization details you provide during registration.
- **Business data** — Invoices, inventory records, customer information, expenses, staff records, and other data you enter into BusinessOS to run your business.
- **Usage data** — Log data such as IP address, browser type, device information, and pages accessed for security, debugging, and service improvement.
- **Authentication** — Session cookies and tokens to keep you signed in securely.

## How we use information

We use your information to:

- Provide and maintain the BusinessOS service
- Process billing and subscription management
- Send transactional emails (verification, password reset, billing notices)
- Improve product reliability and security
- Respond to support requests sent to admin@econsole.in

We do not sell your business data to third parties.

## Data storage and security

Business data is stored on secure cloud infrastructure. We use encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and industry-standard access controls. Organization data is isolated per tenant — users only access organizations they belong to.

Offline Android data is stored locally on your device and synchronized to our servers when connectivity is available.

## Cookies and sessions

We use essential cookies for authentication and session management. These are required for the application to function and are not used for third-party advertising.

## Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data by contacting **admin@econsole.in**. Organization owners can export business data through the application. Upon account deletion, we will remove personal data within a reasonable timeframe, subject to legal retention requirements.

## Third-party services

We may use third-party providers for email delivery, payment processing, cloud hosting, and analytics. These providers process data only as needed to deliver the service and under contractual obligations.

## Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.

## Contact

Privacy questions: **admin@econsole.in**
