# Pricing — BusinessOS by E-console

> Simple monthly plans for Indian retail and service businesses. Contact us for yearly or multi-shop rates.

BusinessOS by E-console offers tiered monthly plans based on shop size and features. All plans include GST-ready billing, inventory, and web access. Higher tiers unlock multi-branch, staff modules, contractor/architect tools, and priority support.

## Plans overview

Plans are designed for single-shop starters through multi-branch operations. Yearly billing and custom enterprise arrangements are available on request — email admin@econsole.in.

## What's included

- GST invoice generation and printing
- Inventory and purchase management
- Customer ledger (udhaar)
- Staff attendance (plan-dependent)
- Android offline billing app
- Windows desktop client

## Contact

Questions about plans, yearly billing, or more than one shop? Visit [/contact](/contact) or email admin@econsole.in.
