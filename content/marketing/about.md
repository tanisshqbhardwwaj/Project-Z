# About E-console and BusinessOS

> E-console builds BusinessOS — an all-in-one business management platform for Indian businesses on econsole.in.

## Who we are

E-console is the company behind BusinessOS, a software platform designed for the way Indian shops and small businesses actually operate. We focus on GST-ready billing, inventory control, customer credit (udhaar), staff management, and industry-specific modules for contractors, architects, and service providers.

Our tagline — **Powering Digital Possibilities** — reflects our goal: give every business owner professional tools without enterprise complexity or cost.

## What BusinessOS does

BusinessOS replaces scattered spreadsheets, manual registers, and disconnected apps with one system:

- **Retail & wholesale shops** run billing, stock, purchases, returns, and customer ledgers from a single dashboard. The Android app works offline for counter billing; data syncs when connectivity returns.
- **Multi-branch operators** manage inventory transfers, branch-specific settings, and consolidated reporting.
- **Contractors** track BOQ (bill of quantities), material usage, and project-linked expenses.
- **Architects** manage project stages and documentation alongside billing.
- **Service businesses** handle appointments, contracts, packages, and commissions.

Plans scale from single-shop starters to organizations with multiple branches and module add-ons.

## Built for India

BusinessOS is built around Indian business practices: GST invoicing, udhaar credit tracking, rupee-first pricing, and support hours aligned to IST (Monday–Saturday, 9:00 AM–7:00 PM).
